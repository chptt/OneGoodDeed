# OneGoodDeed – Product
OneGoodDeed is a donation platform where donors give to verified, registered organisations (NGOs, charities, trusts, foundations, societies). It was migrated from an older project called "DonFan", which let donors support influencers. All influencer concepts are being removed or replaced.

## Principles
- Trust first: only verified organisations are public and can receive donations.
- Transparent: show registration number, verified badge, funds raised, donor count, and how funds are used.
- Standard, professional UX in the style of mainstream fundraising platforms. No novelty theming.

## Naming
- Product name: OneGoodDeed (package/repo/slug: onegooddeed).
- Never use "DonFan", "donfan", or "influencer" in code, UI, comments, or docs.
